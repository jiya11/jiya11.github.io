Align

Overview

Align is an in-progress tool that translates physiological biomarker data into real time insights and guided coping strategies through a chatbot, enabling users to better understand and manage mental health crises outside therapy sessions. Our goal is to strengthen the mind-body connection, providing personalized insights and continuous support to empower users in their mental health journey.

As part of UofT's Hatchery program, our team has developed a business plan grounded in real-world feedback. We conducted market research and interviews with healthcare professionals and individuals with lived experience to ensure our solution addresses genuine needs. On the technical side, we're exploring the use of physiological biomarkers (such as Heart Rate Variability (HRV) and Electrodermal Activity (EDA)) with a personalized chatbot to provide tailored mental health guidance and support for each user.

Our work is ongoing, with a focus on combining cutting-edge technology, user-centered design, and scientific research to create a meaningful tool for mental well-being.
